This walkthrough assumes abstract is [installed](/docs/install/).

<Steps>

1. **Open a workspace.** A workspace is just a folder. Create one and open it:

   ```bash
   mkdir ~/my-review
   abstract ~/my-review
   ```

   Your browser opens at `http://localhost:4477` with an empty chat. (You can also create
   workspaces later from the **Workspaces** screen; those go in `~/Abstract/`.)

2. **Connect a model.** Open **Settings** in the sidebar and either:
   - click **Continue with ChatGPT** to use your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, or
   - click **+ Add provider** and paste an API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or OpenRouter),
     or point it at a local Ollama server.

   See [Connect a model](/docs/models/) for details.

3. **Add your papers (optional).** In the chat box, click **+** → **Save files to workspace** and
   pick PDFs. They're saved to `sources/` and appear in the **Files** panel on the right. You can
   also copy files into the folder yourself — the panel refreshes every few seconds.

   Files are *not* indexed automatically: the agent ingests what it needs, or you can ask it to
   ("ingest my PDFs").

4. **Ask for something.** Write in plain words, for example:

   > Do a literature review on retrieval-augmented generation for question answering, then write
   > a verified related-work section.

   The agent shows its plan, searches OpenAlex, Crossref, and arXiv, fetches open-access PDFs,
   reads them, and drafts. You can keep typing while it works — it reads your message at its next
   step — or press the stop button.

5. **Read the draft.** The draft appears in the chat with each cited sentence colored by its
   verdict: green for <span class="verdict ok">supported</span>, amber for
   <span class="verdict partial">partial</span>, red for <span class="verdict bad">unsupported</span>.
   Hover a sentence to see the exact quote behind it; click to open the source at that page.

6. **Revise and export.** Ask for changes ("tighten the second paragraph", "fix the unsupported
   sentences") — the same document is revised in place. When you're happy, click **Export** on the
   draft. You get Markdown, LaTeX, BibTeX, and an audit file in `drafts/`.

</Steps>

## What next

- Learn what the verdicts mean in [Verified drafting](/docs/verified-drafting/).
- See how searching and screening work in [Literature search](/docs/literature-search/).
- Keep a review going over several sessions — conversations are saved per workspace, and the agent
  can search your earlier ones.
